Guesswork vs. Strategy

Most applicants guess. You don't have to.

Top Singapore universities are highly selective. The difference between applicants is rarely marks alone. It's the plan behind them.

😕 Applying on guesswork

  • Picking a university by ranking or by what friends chose
  • Hoping good board marks will be enough
  • Adding random activities in Class 12 to "fill" the profile
  • Discovering deadlines and documents at the last minute
  • Choosing a course without checking subject requirements

🎯 Applying with clarity

  • Shortlisting NUS, NTU, SMU or SUTD based on real fit
  • Knowing exactly what admissions look for beyond marks
  • Building a focused profile from Class 6 to 12
  • Following a clear timeline with documents planned early
  • Matching subject choices to the intended course

02 Admissions Expectations

What does it really take to stand out?

Good marks matter. But a strong application is about more than marks. Understand the factors to prepare for, including:

  • Academic performance
  • Subject choices
  • Achievements
  • Competitions
  • Extracurricular activities
  • Projects and experiences
  • Overall student profile
03 Profile Building

Your child's profile doesn't start with the application form.

The strongest profiles are built over time. Learn what students can start doing during Class 6 to Class 12 to develop experiences that align with their intended course and career direction.
