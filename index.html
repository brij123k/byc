Planning for Singapore Universities?

Don't leave your child's university application to chance.

Singapore has some of Asia's most competitive universities, including:

  • National University of Singapore (NUS)
  • Nanyang Technological University (NTU)
  • Singapore Management University (SMU)
  • Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD)

But knowing the names is only the beginning.

The real questions are:

  • Which university is the right fit for your child?
  • What do these universities actually look for?
  • Are grades alone enough?
  • How important are extracurriculars and achievements?
  • What should students do before they apply?
  • How should they build their profile?
  • When should the application strategy begin?

This webinar will help you answer these questions before it's too late.

02 Admissions Expectations

What does it really take to stand out?

Good marks matter. But a strong application is about more than marks. Understand the factors students should consider while preparing for competitive Singapore university applications, including:

  • Academic performance
  • Subject choices
  • Achievements
  • Competitions
  • Extracurricular activities
  • Projects and experiences
  • Overall student profile
03 Profile Building

Your child's profile doesn't start with the application form.

The strongest profiles are built over time. Learn what students can start doing during Grades 9, 10, 11 and 12 to develop experiences that align with their intended course and career direction.

Discover:
