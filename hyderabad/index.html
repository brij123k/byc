Where to Apply

Same country. Very different universities.

Each of Singapore's leading universities has its own strengths, teaching style and idea of the ideal student. Applying to the right one starts with knowing the difference.

NUS National University of Singapore

Singapore's flagship comprehensive university, with a broad range of faculties and a strong research culture.

Often suits students who want breadth and academic depth.

NTU Nanyang Technological University

A research-intensive university known especially for engineering, science and technology.

Often suits students drawn to STEM and hands-on innovation.

SMU Singapore Management University

A city-campus university focused on business, economics, law and computing, with small seminar-style classes.

Often suits students who enjoy discussion, presenting and business.

SUTD Singapore University of Technology and Design

A design-centred university that blends technology, engineering and architecture through interdisciplinary projects.

Often suits makers and builders who learn by designing.

But "suits" isn't the same as "fits your profile". That's what we'll work out together in Hyderabad.

Planning for Singapore Universities?

Don't leave your child's university application to chance.

Singapore has some of Asia's most competitive universities. But knowing their names is only the beginning.

The right choice depends on your child's profile, course and goals.

The real questions are:

  • Which university is the right fit for your child?
  • What do these universities actually look for?
  • Are grades alone enough?
  • How important are extracurriculars and achievements?
  • What should students do before they apply?
  • How should they build their profile?
  • When should the application strategy begin?

This session will help you answer these questions before it's too late.
